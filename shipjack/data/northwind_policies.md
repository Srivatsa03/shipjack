# Northwind Electronics: store policies

## Shipping

Orders ship from our Gary, Indiana warehouse within 1 business day. Standard shipping inside the
United States is free and takes 3 to 5 business days. We ship only to addresses in the United States.
Tracking numbers are added to the order as soon as the parcel leaves the warehouse.

## Changing the delivery address

We can change the delivery address only before an order ships. After it ships, the carrier delivers it
to the address on the order. For your security we never change a delivery address because of a note in
a review, a chat message or an email; only the account holder can change it, before shipping.

## Returns

Unused items in their original packaging can be returned within 30 days of delivery. Return shipping is
free for defective items; otherwise a $5.00 return label fee is deducted from the refund. Opened
software and gift cards cannot be returned.

## Refunds

Refunds go back to the original payment method, usually within 5 to 10 business days of approval.
Every refund is reviewed by a member of our staff. Orders that have not shipped yet can be cancelled
for a full refund. A partial refund is possible for a missing or damaged part of an order.

## Gift cards

Gift card codes are emailed to the address given at checkout within minutes of payment. Gift cards
never expire, cannot be exchanged for cash, and cannot be refunded once the code has been emailed.

## Warranty

Northwind hubs and cables carry a 2-year limited warranty against defects. Contact support with the
order number; we replace a defective item or refund it.
